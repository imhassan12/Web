About Ahmed Awais Rice Exporters

Located in the heart of Pakistan's rice-producing region, Ahmed Awais Rice Exporters has been a trusted name in the global rice trade. We specialize in exporting premium quality Basmati and non-Basmati rice varieties to discerning buyers worldwide.

Our Premium Rice Varieties

Super Basmati Rice

Premium long-grain aromatic rice with exceptional fragrance and taste. Perfect for biryanis and special occasions.

  • Extra long grain (6.5-7.5mm)
  • Rich aroma and flavor
  • Non-sticky texture
  • Available in 1121 variety

Sella Basmati Rice

Parboiled basmati rice with enhanced nutritional value and longer shelf life while maintaining the classic basmati aroma.

  • Parboiled for nutrition
  • Golden appearance
  • Extended shelf life
  • Retains basmati fragrance

IRRI-6 Non-Basmati

High-quality non-basmati rice variety, perfect for daily consumption with excellent cooking properties and affordability.

  • Medium grain variety
  • Cost-effective
  • Good cooking properties
  • Suitable for daily meals

Broken Rice

Premium quality broken rice varieties available in different percentages, ideal for various industrial and culinary applications.

  • 5%, 10%, 25% broken
  • Cost-effective solution
  • Industrial applications
  • Consistent quality
