Our Premium Rice Varieties

Super Basmati

Premium long-grain aromatic rice with exceptional fragrance and taste. Perfect for biryanis and special occasions.

  • Extra long grain (7-8mm)
  • Rich aroma and flavor
  • Non-sticky texture
  • Premium export quality

1121 Basmati

World's longest basmati rice grain with superior cooking quality and exceptional aroma that enhances every dish.

  • Longest grain (8.3mm+)
  • Excellent elongation
  • Premium basmati aroma
  • High market demand

Basmati 385

Traditional basmati variety known for its distinct aroma and consistent quality, ideal for everyday cooking and export.

  • Traditional variety
  • Aromatic basmati
  • Good elongation
  • Cost-effective option

PK 386

High-quality Pakistani rice variety with excellent grain characteristics and superior cooking properties for export markets.

  • Long grain variety
  • Excellent cooking quality
  • Good aroma
  • Popular in Middle East

Basmati D-98 / PK-198

Versatile basmati variety combining quality and affordability, suitable for various international markets and applications.

  • Dual variety benefits
  • Good grain length
  • Aromatic quality
  • Competitive pricing

IRRI-9

High-yielding non-basmati variety with excellent cooking properties, perfect for daily consumption and bulk export.

  • Medium grain
  • High yield variety
  • Good cooking quality
  • Affordable pricing

IRRI-6

Popular non-basmati rice with consistent quality and excellent value, widely exported to African and Asian markets.

  • Medium grain variety
  • High demand globally
  • Good cooking properties
  • Best value for money

KS-282

Premium non-basmati variety with superior grain quality, ideal for markets requiring consistent and reliable rice supply.

  • Long grain non-basmati
  • Excellent quality
  • Good appearance
  • Strong export potential

Brown Rice

Nutritious whole grain rice with natural bran layer intact, perfect for health-conscious markets and premium buyers.

  • Whole grain nutrition
  • High fiber content
  • Rich in vitamins
  • Premium health market
